Our story

We make the windows — start to finish.

Better windows. Better spaces. Better living.

Prominance is a manufacturer, not a reseller. PWDS Extrusions Pvt Ltd (formerly Prominance Window Systems) has been manufacturing since 2011 in Sulur, Coimbatore, with an annual production capacity of 22,000 MT.

We blend, extrude and test our own profiles, then make them into windows and doors and fit them ourselves. That means the quality of the window in your home is decided in one place, by one team — not passed between middlemen.

Our promise is in our name: Designed for India. Engineered to endure. Every profile is built for Indian heat, monsoon and noise, and tested to EN 12608 before it leaves our plant.

About Prominance — your questions, answered

Prominance windows and doors are made by PWDS Extrusions Pvt Ltd (formerly Prominance Window Systems) at its plant in Sulur, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. The company has been manufacturing since 2011 and has an annual production capacity of 22,000 MT.

Our manufacturing plant and corporate head office are at SF. No. 207/1 B & 1 C, Selakaraichal Road, Appanaickenpatti, Sulur, Coimbatore – 641 402, Tamil Nadu, India.

Our uPVC profiles are tested to EN 12608 in our in-house lab, which uses European and Japanese equipment and is backed by R&D, 3D printing and prototyping. Profiles are also tested for 25,000+ hours of weather-ability.
