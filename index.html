Frequently asked questions

Choose uPVC when comfort and low upkeep matter most — bedrooms, living rooms, busy roads and hot afternoons. Choose aluminium when you want slim frames, big glass and wide sliding or folding openings. Many homes use both. Tell us about each room and our team will recommend the right system.

uPVC typically costs less upfront than a comparable aluminium or timber window, and the savings continue afterwards — better insulation lowers energy bills and the frame is virtually maintenance-free. The exact price depends on size, configuration, glass and finish, so request a free quote for your openings.

A uPVC window can last anywhere from a few years to a couple of decades depending on the raw material, steel reinforcement and hardware, so the warranty tells you more than any claim. Prominance laminated finishes carry a 20-year warranty, and our aluminium systems carry a 25-year warranty against surface corrosion.

Yes. Our uPVC profiles are reinforced with 1.5 mm hot-dip galvanised steel, corners are fusion-welded rather than screwed or glued, and doors use multi-point locking (2–3 points). Any door is only as secure as its glass, so we also offer laminated glass, which holds together if someone tries to break in.

Yes. Multi-chambered uPVC with dual EPDM gaskets and the right glazing reduces outside noise by up to 40 dB and typically cuts energy loss by about 30%. The profiles are tested for 25,000+ hours of accelerated weathering by SKZ Germany, and uPVC does not rust, rot or swell — so heat, sun and monsoon leave it unchanged.
